Selected to present at the prestigious 75th Lindau Nobel Laureate Meeting, where young scientists meet Nobel laureates for scientific exchange. This talk demonstrates how models originally developed for tracking radionuclide gas seepage from underground nuclear tests can provide insights into methane release mechanisms on Mars.
