**Preprint** (Also submitted to *The Astrophysical Journal Letters*)

**Abstract:**
> We present detailed observational planning for the anticipated impact of a Falcon 9 upper stage on the lunar surface on August 5, 2026. This provides a unique opportunity to study impact processes on the Moon and test observational capabilities for detecting and characterizing future impact events.
