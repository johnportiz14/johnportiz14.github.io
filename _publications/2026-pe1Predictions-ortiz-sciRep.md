**Abstract:**
> We present predictions of multiphase flow and tracer transport for the Physics Experiment 1 (PE1), an underground chemical explosive test at the Nevada National Security Site. This work combines numerical simulations with field observations to validate models of gas migration following subsurface explosions, with important implications for nuclear test monitoring and verification.
